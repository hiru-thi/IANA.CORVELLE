About the house

We make small things meant to outlast the year they were bought in.

— Iana Corvelle, founder

IANA.CORVELLE began as a single notebook, cut and bound by hand on a kitchen table, because nothing on the shelf felt worth keeping. Every piece since has followed the same rule: if it won't matter in ten years, we don't make it.

Each cover is debossed, never printed. Each page is chosen for weight before it's chosen for price. We work with two binderies, both family-run, both older than the brand itself.
