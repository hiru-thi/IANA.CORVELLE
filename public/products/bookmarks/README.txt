BOOKMARKS — drop your product images here
─────────────────────────────────────────
Accepted formats: .jpg  .jpeg  .png  .webp

Name each file to match the product slug in src/data.ts:
  thin-gold-line.jpg     → "Thin Gold Line"
  aperture.jpg           → "Aperture"

Image tips:
  • Best size: 400 × 1200 px (tall portrait — bookmarks are narrow)
  • Keep file size under 300 KB for fast loading
  • A clean white or dark background shows the bookmark clearly
