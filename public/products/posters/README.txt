POSTERS — drop your product images here
────────────────────────────────────────
Accepted formats: .jpg  .jpeg  .png  .webp

Name each file to match the product slug in src/data.ts:
  low-tide.jpg           → "Low Tide"
  still-frame.jpg        → "Still Frame"

Image tips:
  • Best size: 800 × 1100 px (portrait) or 1100 × 800 px (landscape)
  • Keep file size under 500 KB for fast loading
  • For posters, a flat-lay or framed mockup works best
